This policy explains how the VYBE (جوهر) mobile app ("VYBE", "we") handles information. VYBE is a personality app: you answer statements and receive a written personality portrait.

1. Your answers and portraits stay on your device

Your assessment answers, your personality portraits and the app's internal results are stored only in a local database on your device. We do not run servers that receive them, we do not require an account, and we do not upload your answers or portraits to us or to any advertising network.

The app also stores your language, theme and similar settings on your device, plus simple usage events (for example, "assessment completed"). These events stay on your device. They never include your answers or your portrait text.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

VYBE is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interactions. Google processes this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Where required (for example, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

3. Sharing

If you choose to share a card or a link, the app creates an image on your device and opens your device's share sheet. Share cards contain only your portrait text, never your answers or any scores. What happens next depends on the app you share to.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, photos or precise location.
  • We do not use your answers for advertising.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete all assessments, portraits and local usage events at any time in Settings → Delete assessment data. Uninstalling the app also removes this data from your device. Because we do not hold copies, there is nothing further for us to delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, use Google's controls listed above.

6. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security. Keep your device locked if others can access it.

7. Children

VYBE is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Not a medical service

VYBE is a self-description tool. It is not a diagnosis, therapy, or a basis for clinical, hiring or relationship decisions.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

  • سياسة خصوصية Google

حيث يلزم ذلك (مثل المنطقة الاقتصادية الأوروبية والمملكة المتحدة وسويسرا)، يطلب التطبيق موافقتك عبر رسالة الموافقة من Google، ويمكنك تغيير اختيارك لاحقًا من الإعدادات ← خيارات خصوصية الإعلانات. ويمكنك أيضًا إعادة تعيين معرّف الإعلانات أو حذفه أو إيقاف الإعلانات المخصصة من إعدادات جهازك.
